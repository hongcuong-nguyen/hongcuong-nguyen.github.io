PV-AgentIE: Auditing Constraint-Guided Agentic Information Extraction for Electrochemical Knowledge Bases
Manuscript · 2026
Nguyen Hong Cuong, et al. · VinUniversity (VINMATICS-SC Project)

A two-agent cross-section table–text extraction and physics-verification workflow auditing checker evasion vs. genuine repair and faithful abstention across 3,430 supercapacitor papers, Eval-50/Bench-200 benchmarks, and six LLM backbones.

Project Page (VINMATICS-SC)
Abstract: Scientific extraction agents pair large language models (LLMs) with executable domain checks, yet external validator feedback can induce superficial compliance (checker evasion) rather than source-faithful repair. We present and audit PV-AgentIE, coupling full-context cross-section extraction with deterministic electrochemical verification (E–C–ΔV conservation and 3E/2E isolation) and two-round rationale-logged reflection. On human-verified Eval-50 (397 gold records), PV-AgentIE achieves 87.1% F1 (+8.0 pp over paired Pre-Critic) with zero checker evasions, while exposing three distinct evasion mechanisms across five of six audited LLM backbones and programmatic heuristics.
